FlightWave Edge 130 / Edge 130 Blue

A production, military/ISR-grade tilt-rotor tricopter with proprietary tilt-pod technology — proof this configuration scales all the way up to fielded, contracted hardware, not just hobby and research builds.

Configuration

FlightWave (now under Red Cat Holdings) describes the Edge 130's motor arrangement around what it calls proprietary "tilt-pod technology" — two tilting motor pods plus one fixed motor, the same 2-tilt-plus-1-fixed layout as every other design surveyed here, applied at production scale for a fielded military/ISR product.

Airframe & body design

130cm × 77cm, 1200g all-up weight, 60+ minute endurance, 15 m/s (33 mph) cruise, 20km range, powered by an 86.4 Wh / 6.0 Ah / 14.8V battery. Construction details (materials, fabrication method) aren't published anywhere found during this research.

What's distinctive

Tool-free payload swapping between two payload options (a 4K/IR overwatch camera, or three synchronized 13MP mapping cameras), AES-256 encrypted radios, and marketing as made-in-USA military-grade hardware. Red Cat Holdings has reported government contracts for the Edge 130 Blue worth $518K and $1M in bulk orders — this is real, deployed hardware, not a concept or prototype.

Flight controller & software

FlightWave's own site references a proprietary "FlightWave Mission Planner" rather than describing the autopilot as PX4. A PX4.io project page for "flywave-edge" does exist, but its content doesn't itself confirm PX4 as the production flight-control software — treat that listing as unconfirmed rather than as proof the fielded aircraft runs PX4.

Cost

Not publicly priced. Only institutional/government contract values were found ($518K and $1M reported bulk-order figures) — there's no public per-unit consumer price.
