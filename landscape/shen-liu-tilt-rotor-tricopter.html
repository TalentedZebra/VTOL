Shen & Liu tilt-rotor tri-copter (2025)

A 2025 conference paper describing a flying-wing tilt-rotor tri-copter configuration with a claimed fabricated and flight-tested prototype — the newest design surveyed in this section.

Sourcing caveat, stated up front: every attempt to directly load this paper (both the MDPI page and a ResearchGate mirror, tried twice each) returned a 403 error. Nothing below has been confirmed by actually reading the paper — it's derived from search-indexed abstract text only. Read the paper directly before citing anything here as settled fact.

Configuration

Per search-indexed abstract text: "two front rotors that can tilt... the third rotor is fixed in the aft fuselage" — the same 2-tilt-plus-1-fixed layout as the rest of this section, but this specific claim has not been independently verified by reading the source.

Airframe & body design

Described in the abstract as a "flying-wing configuration." No wingspan, weight, or other dimensional specs were found in any source consulted.

What's distinctive

Per the abstract, the paper covers airfoil selection and aerodynamic-parameter calculation specifically for this flying-wing tilt-rotor tri-copter shape, alongside a custom tilting mechanism design. It was presented at the 2nd International Conference on Green Aviation (ICGA 2024) in Chengdu, November 2024, and published in MDPI's Engineering Proceedings in 2025.

Flight controller & software

Classical PID flight control, per the abstract — no specific hardware or firmware platform named.

Cost

Research prototype — no pricing information found or expected.

A note on the flight-test claim

The abstract states a prototype "was fabricated and subjected to flight tests" confirming the design's performance via simulation and flight testing. This claim comes only from search-indexed abstract text, not a direct reading of the paper's actual results section — treat it as reported-but-unverified until the source is read directly.
