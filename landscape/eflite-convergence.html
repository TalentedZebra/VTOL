E-flite Convergence

A ready-to-fly, commercially manufactured tilt-rotor tricopter — and notable for being the design PX4 itself ships an official reference configuration for.

Configuration

Three motors total: left and right wingtip motors sit on nacelles actuated by two tilt servos, while the back (tail) motor is fixed. PX4's own documentation channel-maps this as two tilting nacelles plus one fixed motor — the same 2-tilt-plus-1-fixed layout as every other entry in this section, despite some of PX4's own phrasing describing "three fixed motors... paired with two tilt servos" in a way that reads ambiguously out of context.

Airframe & body design

650mm wingspan, 680mm length, 771g flying weight. Foam construction with carbon-fiber reinforcement, in keeping with typical ready-to-fly hobby foam aircraft.

What's distinctive

This is the only design surveyed here sold as a complete, ready-to-fly commercial product rather than a kit, a set of files, or a research prototype — and PX4 maintains an official "E-flite Convergence" VTOL tiltrotor frame class and reference wiki page for it, making it a common starting point for hobbyists who want to run PX4 on a proven commercial tricopter airframe rather than build one from scratch.

Flight controller & software

Stock units ship with a proprietary flight controller. The aftermarket path documented by PX4 itself replaces this with a Pixhawk-class board — originally the (now discontinued) Pixfalcon, with Pixhawk 4 Mini or Holybro Pixhawk Mini given as current replacements, selecting the "E-flite Convergence" VTOL Tiltrotor frame class in QGroundControl.

Cost

No current retail price was confirmed during this research — Horizon Hobby has listed the Convergence historically, but check their current listing directly rather than relying on an old figure, since retail availability and pricing for this product move.
